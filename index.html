About Me

Translational Oncology & Evidence-Based Innovation

I am a final-year medical student at the Services Institute of Medical Sciences (SIMS), Lahore, Pakistan, with a strong academic foundation (USMLE Step 1 Cleared) and a dedicated interest in oncology, particularly in the development and clinical application of targeted cancer therapies.

My research experience spans systematic reviews, meta-analyses, and real-world data studies related to internal medicine and targeted oncology. Beyond research, I am actively engaged in medical leadership and humanitarian initiatives through societies like IFMSA Pakistan and VFAHT Pakistan.
