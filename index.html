Muhammad Ansab MBBS Final Year

Clinical Care at Services Institute of Medical Sciences (SIMS), Oncology Research, and Evidence-Based Innovation

I am a final-year medical student at the Services Institute of Medical Sciences (SIMS) with a strong academic foundation and a dedicated interest in oncology, particularly in the development and clinical application of targeted cancer therapies.

My research experience spans systematic reviews, meta-analyses, and real-world data studies related to internal medicine and targeted oncology. I am especially drawn to translational oncology and precision medicine, and I aspire to contribute to the advancement of biomarker-driven treatment strategies in solid tumors.

With a growing portfolio of scientific presentations and publications, I aim to build a career at the intersection of clinical care, oncology research, and evidence-based innovation. Beside I have been involved in various volunteer activities through societies like IFMSA Pakistan and VFAHT Pakistan.
